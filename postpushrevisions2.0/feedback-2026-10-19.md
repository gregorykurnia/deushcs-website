## F31. Simulation tile missing from the GBA spotlight

**Cause:** `gbaSpotlight.games` in `src/content/en/home.ts` lists only Personality, Cognitive and Neuroscience. The mockup had three tiles; R2 added Simulation as a fourth module (it's in the tags) but not its tile.

**Proposal:** add a fourth tile, Simulation, captioned "Business Operations". There's no Simulation screenshot yet, so the tile shows the missing-screen placeholder (`gameBusinessOperations`, DESIGN-GUIDE §14.3) until the owner sends one (TODO-NEXT item 4). The row is re-gridded for four tiles (now `1.6fr 1fr 1fr`); Personality stays the lead tile with the event log over it.

## F32. Recognition captions

**Cause:** the three captions use three different shapes: "Batch 6 (2023): one of 17 startups selected by Kominfo, now Komdigi", "Singapore (2024): represented Komdigi" and just "2024" (the Round 1 review flagged the last one).

**Proposal:** every caption is the year, then one short sentence, reworded from About's timeline and `Creating DEUS HCS Website.md`, with no claims beyond the source:

- Startup Studio Indonesia: "2023 · Selected for Batch 6, one of 17 startups chosen by Kominfo (now Komdigi)."
- Innovfest by NUS x AsiaTech: "2024 · Represented Komdigi in Singapore."
- Tech in Asia: "2024 · Took part in Tech in Asia." (the source only says DEUS "took part")

## F33. Uneven solution tiles

**Cause:** not a deliberate choice. The product-tile grid is `1.25fr 1fr` (DESIGN-GUIDE §7, "Product tiles"), made for the old layout of one lead tile next to two small ones. R2 made GBA and Enhance two equal lead tiles side by side but kept the ratio.

**Decision:** make them equal (`1fr 1fr`); Potentia stays full width below.
