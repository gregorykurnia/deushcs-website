# Client logos (collected 2026-10-23, not on the site)

Logos for the client logo wall (Q8), collected from the owner's client list. They sit here, outside `public/`, so none of them can go live until the owner approves which clients may be shown. Approved logos move to `public/assets/clients/` and the wall is switched on in `src/content/flags.ts`.

- Sources, confidence and how each company was identified: `sources-*.md` (one file per batch). The sizes in those files are from before trimming. Since then, transparent padding was trimmed from every PNG, files over 2400px wide were scaled down, and Colorbox, Tirajeans and Wood were rebuilt from Delami's larger white versions, recoloured black. The `delami/*-white-*.png` files are those white versions.
- SPH is one logo for all campuses (owner, 2026-10-23).
