## Top bar

### F1. Solutions menu hierarchy

> When hovering over "Solutions" it seems awkward: Game-Based Assessment, DEUS Discover and Next Chapter are supposed to be under "Assess", but the way "Assess" is displayed makes it look like it's on the same level. Same for "Perform and reward".

> Follow-up (owner, 2026-10-16, with a screenshot of the menu): two levels of nesting would be odd. If "Assess" is there, "Game-Based Assessment" doesn't need to be there too, but it should be clear that assessing happens *through* game-based assessment. The headings also look like clickable products. Maybe drop "Assess" and "Perform and reward" and go straight to the products?

**Response:** Agreed. The headings use exactly the same style as the products (bold title plus a grey line), so the menu reads as six products, two of them fake links. Dropping the group headings fixes both problems:

- **No "Assess" or "Perform and reward" headings.** Each column starts with the product itself.
- **Game-Based Assessment leads its column**, and the verb moves into its description, so "assess through game-based assessment" is said in the menu: *"Assess candidates and employees through games."* DEUS Discover and Next Chapter sit under it with a thin left rule as its two parts. That's the one real level of nesting, the same as F10.
- **DEUS Enhance** leads the next column the same way: *"Run fair appraisals and pay for real performance."* (the current "Perform and reward" line, which moves into the product).
- **Potentia** goes below Discover and Next Chapter in the Game-Based Assessment column (owner, 2026-10-16), as its own card, not nested, with its "opens deuspotentia.com" note.
- The panel gets narrower (about 680px instead of 880px), since it now has two columns instead of three (owner, 2026-10-16).
- The mobile menu gets the same shape.

### F2. Hero headline

> "Hire on Evidence, Reward on Evidence": not strong enough.

**Response:** Agreed. It's clever, but it talks about "evidence" before the reader knows what DEUS does. This is also open question Q3. Options, all within what the site already claims:

It's best decided on a sample. Claude can make a styleguide page showing 1 and 3 side by side before anything changes on the live pages.
