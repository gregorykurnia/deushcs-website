Section labels: now, and the options

Feedback F4. Every section header on the site starts with a small label in a round pill ("Why DEUS exists", "Where we help"). Each option below shows the same two Home sections with their real wording: one on a white section, one on a sky section. Headings are shown a little smaller than on the site (34px instead of 52px) so the options fit side by side; the label sizes are the real ones.

Option 1: plain text label, no pill

Recommended · chosen

Same wording and colour (azure-ink), no background or rounded shape, and a bit closer to the heading (12px instead of 20px) so it reads as part of it. The quietest change, and it still helps people scanning the page.

White section
Why DEUS exists

Most people decisions still run on thin evidence.

Companies say people are their most important asset. Then they make decisions about them with the weakest data they have.

Sky section
Where we help

One partner, from the first assessment to the last bonus decision.
