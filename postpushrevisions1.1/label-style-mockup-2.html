Section labels, round two

Feedback F4. Two things made the first round look off. The first mockup shrank the headings to 34px but kept the labels at 14px, so the proportions weren't the site's. And on the site, a 14px label under a 52px heading is very small and easy to lose. This round shows everything at the real desktop size (52px headings), with slightly larger labels and accents that balance on a centred heading. Each option uses the real "Why DEUS exists" section from Home.

A. Lines on both sides

Recommended

Your 3a, fixed: a short line on each side, so the label sits centred over the heading instead of leaning left. The lines are thinner and lighter than in 3a, and the text is a little bigger (15px instead of 14px). Keeps sentence case, so it stays within the design guide.
