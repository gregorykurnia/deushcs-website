## 5. Product deep-dives

### 5.1 Game-Based Assessment suite (Discover + Next Chapter)

**Problems it solves (pain points):**
1. Candidates study how to "pass" personality tests, so results reflect preparation rather than character.
2. Abstract cognitive tests fail to show how someone thinks under pressure.
3. Many candidates drop out of long, boring assessments before finishing.
4. HR teams spend days scoring and compiling results manually.

**Three positioning pillars:**
- **Real behavior signals** — Candidates cannot fake how they play. Every choice and hesitation becomes data.
- **Scientifically grounded** — Built on Big Five, neuroscience, and cognitive psychology frameworks.
- **Enterprise-ready** — Modular, scalable, managed through a CMS for high-volume hiring.

**Use cases ("What's our assessment for"):**
| Use case | Description |
|---|---|
| Annual Management Trainee (MT) recruitment | High volume; shortlist quickly and fairly. Cognitive + Neuro for first-pass screening, Simulation for the finalist round. |
| Competency mapping | Match individual profiles to role competency frameworks, identify gaps, build targeted development plans. |
| Leadership identification programs | Internal talent for supervisor/manager pipeline. Neuro module's risk, motivation, and EQ data is uniquely suited. |
| Internal talent mapping | Visualize capability distribution; Cognitive + Personality data maps strengths, gaps, hidden high-potentials across teams/divisions. |
| Campus recruitment | Large applicant pools across universities; Personality + Cognitive gives a clear differentiated ranking. |
| Talent & development programs | Use data to prioritize L&D investment, personalized paths for HiPos and succession candidates. |

#### Module A — Personality (DEUS Discover)
- Digital **situational judgment** profiling delivered as an **RPG-style narrative game**.
- Measures **32 personality subtraits**, **24 competency traits**, plus **customized competencies**; produces in-depth qualitative analysis.
- Inspired by **Big Five (OCEAN)** personality traits and a **Universal Competency Framework**.
- Differentiators:
  - **Rapid profiling** — comprehensive results same day or next day.
  - **Custom competencies** — measures tailored to organizational needs.
  - **Mass-scale summary** — structured digital dashboards to review, compare, and monitor across groups.
  - **End-to-end usage** — not just recruitment: employee development, promotion readiness, training needs analysis, leadership mapping.
- **Customizable competencies:**
  - Result indicators in *your* organization's language (framed around your competency framework).
  - Competencies mapped to role requirements (e.g., different competencies for Finance, Sales, Marketing).
  - Scenarios, industry context, and business language reflect the client's world.
- Reports include: Personality Reports and an **Individual Profile Suitability** view.

#### Module B — Cognitive (7 games)
| Game | Measures |
|---|---|
| Objective Shooter | Processing speed & precision |
| Reverse Retention | Working memory |
| Odd One Out | Pattern recognition |
| Mallet Math | Arithmetic & problem solving |
| Object Turn | Spatial reasoning |
| Match Blast | Logical reasoning |
| Speed Sorting | Cognitive agility |

- What traditional tests miss: answers can be coached/memorized; no insight into speed or consistency; hard to scale for large hiring volumes.
- What this gives you: real-time accuracy and speed metrics; learning-agility benchmark; unlimited scalability.

#### Module C — Neuroscience (6 games)
Six neuroscience-based mini games capturing **motivation, risk tolerance, effort allocation, and emotional intelligence through unconscious behavior**.
| Game | Measures |
|---|---|
| Balloon Game | Risk taking & learning ability |
| Tower Game | Planning & problem solving |
| Keypress Game | Motivation & processing speed |
| Hard or Easy Task | Effort allocation, decision making |
| Lengths Game | Attention to detail |
| Faces Game | EQ, empathy & emotional recognition |

- What interviews miss: motivation is often misjudged; risk appetite appears too late; EQ is hard to assess fairly.
- What this gives you: behavioral data from actual actions; early visibility into risk profile; emotional-recognition insights for leadership and service roles.

#### Module D — Simulation (fully customizable)
Real business simulations covering case analysis, investigation, reporting, and budget allocation — customizable via the CMS.
- **Game 1: Business Operations** — review cases, investigate information, write a business report. Measures judgment, accuracy, analytical communication.
- **Game 2: Event Operations** — allocate budget under a live timer and generate a report. Measures prioritization, resource allocation, decision speed.
- **Aspects measured:** Analytical Thinking · Financial & Business Acumen · Diagnostic Information Gathering · Written Synthesis.
- What case interviews miss: candidates memorize frameworks; assessment centers are costly and hard to scale; generic cases lack company relevance.
- What this gives you: testing in your industry context; prioritization, accuracy, and analysis under real-time pressure; consistent and scalable.

#### Reports
| Report | Contents | For | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Individual** | Executive summary, overall score + percentile, core competency breakdown, sub-competency analysis, strengths & development areas, final hire recommendation with narrative | Hiring manager making a go/no-go call | Exportable PDF or shareable live link |
| **Batch / Cohort** | Cohort overview, domain averages, full ranked candidate list, cut-off flags, position summaries per role track | HR running MT, campus drives, leadership assessments at volume | Downloadable batch PDF |
| **Interactive Live** | Filterable dashboard; sort by role track, module, score band, cut-off status; click any candidate for full profile | Hiring committees, C-suite talent reviews, panels | Shareable link |

#### Enterprise CMS ("The platform behind everything")
Turns individual assessments into an actual hiring workflow:
1. Bulk participant upload via CSV (no manual entry for MT/campus drives)
2. Email blast and automated reminders
3. Real-time status tracking (started / completed / not opened)
4. Cut-off score system (below-threshold candidates auto-flagged)
5. Auto report generation (individual PDF or full batch in one click)
6. Role-based access control (HR, recruiters, managers)

**Modular deployment:** deploy one module, combine two, or run the full suite. Start with what you need; scale as you see results.

#### Four credibility pillars
1. **Built on established science** — Big Five, neuroscience behavioral paradigms, classical cognitive assessment frameworks.
2. **Expert-guided creation** — 4 phases: Theory → Item Development → Validation → Norming, guided by psychometricians and I/O psychology researchers.
3. **Validated against traditional tools** — convergent validity with traditional cognitive tests.
4. **Normed on the Indonesian population** — local benchmarks, not imported Western norms.

#### Business impact
- **Better hire quality** — behavioral + cognitive data predicts performance better than interviews and resumes alone.
- **Faster hiring cycles** — automated scoring and instant reports; decisions that took days happen the same day.
- **Stronger employer branding** — candidates perceive the company as modern, innovative, talent-serious.
- **Scalable high-volume screening** — run MT and campus at full volume without proportionally increasing HR workload.
- **ROI framing** — one prevented bad hire at manager level typically recovers 5–10× the annual cost of a full assessment program.

#### How it works (4 steps)
1. **Invitation** — bulk upload via CSV; branded invitations sent automatically.
2. **Secure start** — device checks and anti-cheat logic; no downloads required.
3. **Immersive assessment** — personality RPGs, cognitive games, or business simulations.
4. **Instant insights** — scores and interactive reports generated immediately; zero manual grading.

**Implementation benefits:** Anti-cheat proctoring · Frictionless (mobile + desktop browser, no app) · Zero manual overhead · Flexible deployment.

### 5.2 DEUS Enhance — People Performance Platform
**Hero line:** "From Scattered Data to Better Decisions." DEUS Enhance connects your performance data to every KPI, review, and pay decision.
**Closing line:** "Make performance visible. Make decisions clearer."

**Description:** Lets companies monitor employee performance through fully customizable KPI and competency indicators. Integrated with remuneration schemes (bonus, incentives, salary increases) so companies can motivate and reward the right people based on measurable performance evidence.

**Problems:**
- **Scattered data** — KPIs and appraisal results sit across Excel and disconnected systems, slowing consolidation.
- **Inconsistent standards** — managers, departments, or plants evaluate performance differently.
- **Limited visibility** — hard to identify high performers, gaps, and development potential.
- **Disconnected rewards** — results don't consistently inform bonus, incentive, salary, or development decisions.

**"How the gap looks, daily" (persona scenarios — great for a tabbed section):**
- **HR:** It's the week before calibration and HR is still pulling scores from different spreadsheets.
- **Senior Management & Finance:** Bonus gets finalized from a spreadsheet never checked against actual performance ratings.
- **Plant & Operations Leaders:** A plant director asks how their site compares — no one can answer without a week of emails.
- **Employees:** An employee asks why a colleague got a bigger bonus — no one can explain it with objective evidence.
- **Managers & Supervisors:** Two managers rate the same performance differently; nobody notices until calibration, if at all.

**How Enhance closes each gap (6 steps):**
1. **Set the framework** — customize KPIs, competencies, weights, formulas, periods, measures by role and level.
2. **Track across levels** — company → teams → individuals.
3. **Review performance** — one structured self-review and appraisal process for everyone.
4. **Calibrate results** — review evaluations across managers/units for consistency.
5. **Talent mapping** — visualize where everyone sits by performance and potential.
6. **Turn results into decisions** — connect outcomes to incentives, bonuses, salary, development.

**Works with what you have:** complements existing HRIS, ERP, payroll, and operational systems — doesn't require replacing them. Integration and configuration confirmed during scoping.
**Supported beyond the platform:** consultative help with KPI framework design, implementation, user training, post-cycle review, ongoing hands-on assistance.

**Outcomes by persona:**
- HR: one standardized, company-wide framework instead of scattered spreadsheets.
- Plant & Ops leaders: see every team/plant in real time.
- Senior Mgmt & Finance: results connected to incentive, bonus, salary decisions.
- Employees: clarity on expectations and development areas without subjective standards.
- Managers: evaluate consistently; decide with mapping instead of memory or gut feel.

**CTA / next step:** Start with a **current appraisal process review** — a short, low-commitment look at how performance is tracked today. DEUS then recommends a KPI-mapping workshop, a pilot, or a tailored trial. Book a **30-minute call**.

UI screenshot available: "Performance Appraisal Dashboard" (cycle name, completed reviews, ready-to-review, self-reviews, calendar, "My Performance").

### 5.3 Potentia — Child Cognitive Assessment (Potentia by DEUS)
**Primary language for this audience: Bahasa Indonesia.** Tone: warm, empathetic, parent-to-parent.

**What it is:** A gamification-based cognitive profiling platform for children aged **7–15**. Not an academic test, not a boring conventional psychotest — a way to understand how a child's brain works through **8 interactive minigames in ~30 minutes**.
**[VERIFY age range: decks say 7–15; Agate launch article says 9–15.]**

**Hook copy (ID):** "Kita tahu nilai rapor anak. Kita tahu ekskul apa yang dia ikuti. Tapi seberapa banyak dari kita yang benar-benar tahu potensi anak yang belum terlihat?" — "Bukan tentang pintar atau tidak. Tapi tentang cara anak memproses informasi, mengingat, dan belajar…"

**Parent pain points (real quotes from deck, good for a "Sound familiar?" section):**
- "Nanti pas masuk SMA anak mau ambil apa ya? IPA atau IPS?…"
- "Kakaknya bisa belajar sendiri tapi adiknya kok selalu harus didampingi…"
- "Sudah les matematika 2 tahun, tapi sepertinya nilainya segitu-segitu aja."
- "Setiap malam belajar bareng tapi berakhir dengan anak frustrasi dan orangtua ikut emosi."
- "Saya mendaftarkan anak ke lima ekskul sekaligus tapi gak tau yang mana yang benar-benar sesuai."

**Four key features:** Gamified (8 minigames, not rigid multiple choice) · Instant results · Science-based (CHC theory — Cattell–Horn–Carroll) · Actionable (concrete recommendations + option for consultation).

**8 cognitive dimensions:**
| Dimension | What it measures | Relevant for | CHC factor (inferred) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Number Sequence | Recognizing patterns, solving new problems | Math, coding, logic | Gf – Fluid Reasoning |
| Vocabulary | Gathering/organizing information through language | Reading, understanding instructions, writing | Gc – Comprehension Knowledge |
| Visualization | Understanding/processing visual information | Art, architecture, geometry, design | Gv – Visual Processing |
| Memory of Sound Pattern | Interpreting sound patterns | Music, foreign languages, following spoken instructions | Ga – Auditory Processing |
| Complex Working Memory | Holding and processing info while staying focused | Multitasking, long problems, high-pressure exams | Gwm – Working Memory |
| Narration Recall | Recalling verbal information | Learning from teacher explanations, discussions | Gl – Learning Efficiency |
| Semantic Fluency | Accessing information while managing distraction | Class concentration, oral exams, debate | Gr – Retrieval Fluency |
| Rate of Test Taking | Speed on cognitive tasks under time pressure | Timed exams, thinking speed | Gs – Processing Speed |

**8 minigames (names in-game, Indonesian):** Susun Rel · Kotak Misteri · Simbol Kuno · Cerita Ku · Tebak Gambar · Tebak Kata · Harmonisasi Nada · Ramuan Kata.

**What parents receive:** Full cognitive profile (8 dimensions vs. same-age average) · Child's key strengths · Learning style (visual / verbal / hands-on / combination + coaching tips) · Learning strategy recommendations · Extracurricular recommendations · Academic direction signals (IPA/IPS, technical/social, science/arts). Overall score shown as **General Cognitive Ability (GCA)**.

**Report layers:** Instant report (for the child, right after finishing) → **Advanced report** for parents (a few days later) → **Classroom Dashboard** for schools/teachers.

**Benefits for parents:** choose the right tutoring (not the most expensive) · detect learning challenges early · effective ways to support learning at home · healthier expectations and relationship · child feels supported, understood, not judged.

**1-on-1 consultation session:** with a psychology-background consultant — explains each score, gives specific tips (e.g., "use checklists, not long verbal instructions"), extracurricular/tutoring recommendations, long-term direction (e.g., STEM exploration), open Q&A.

**How it works (4 steps):** 1) Parent receives a voucher code → 2) Child enters code; plays on phone or laptop, no app download, just stable internet → 3) Child completes all 8 minigames → 4) Instant report on completion; advanced report after a few days.

**Credibility:** Developed with **Universitas Gadjah Mada (UGM)** involving psychology and education practitioners; based on CHC theory; validated with high validity/reliability; norm-referenced against same-age children (normed for Indonesian children).

**Origin story & partners:** Potentia is the rebranded, modernized evolution of **Batique**, Indonesia's first game-based cognitive assessment (originally developed with PT Melintas Cakrawala Indonesia (MCI), UGM as scientific advisors, and Agate as game developer).
