A game is only useful as an assessment if what it measures is real. This page explains how we make sure it is: the research our assessments are built on, how each one is developed, and who it's benchmarked against.

## 2. The frameworks

Eyebrow: Built on established science

## We didn't invent new theories. We built on proven ones.

**Personality: the Big Five**
DEUS Discover is based on the Big Five (OCEAN) model of personality, the most widely researched personality framework in psychology, together with a Universal Competency Framework that links personality to workplace competencies.

**Behavior: neuroscience paradigms**
The neuroscience module adapts behavioral tasks from neuroscience research, designed to reveal risk-taking, motivation, effort and emotion recognition through what people do rather than what they say.

**Thinking: cognitive assessment**
The cognitive games are grounded in classical frameworks for measuring processing speed, memory, reasoning and attention.

**Children: CHC theory**
Potentia is built on the Cattell–Horn–Carroll (CHC) theory of cognitive abilities, a widely used model for understanding how children think and learn.

## 3. How an assessment is built

Eyebrow: Our process

## Four phases, before a single candidate plays.

Every assessment goes through the same development process, guided by psychometricians and industrial and organizational psychology researchers.

**1. Theory**
We start from what the research says the trait or ability is, and how it shows up in behavior.

**2. Item development**
We design the game mechanics and scenarios that will draw out that behavior.

**3. Validation**
We test whether the game measures what it's meant to, including against traditional tests.

**4. Norming**
We benchmark results against a reference population, so every score means something.

## 4. Validated

Eyebrow: Validation

## Checked against the tests you already trust.

Our cognitive games have been validated against traditional cognitive tests, showing convergent validity: people who score high on one tend to score high on the other. The difference is that ours are faster, more engaging, and much harder to coach for.

## 5. Normed for Indonesia

Eyebrow: Local norms

## Your candidates are compared with people like them.

Many assessments used in Indonesia are benchmarked against Western populations. That means a candidate's score is measured against people with different schooling, different language and different cultural reference points.

Our assessments are normed on the Indonesian population. When a report says a candidate is in the 80th percentile, it means compared with Indonesians, which is the comparison that matters for your hiring.

## 6. Fair and secure
