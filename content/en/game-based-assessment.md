## 2. The problem

Eyebrow: The problem

## Why traditional psychometric tests fall short in recruitment.

**1. Candidates study for it**
Answers to the common personality tests are a quick search away. Results end up measuring how well someone prepared, not who they are.

**2. It can't show thinking under pressure**
Abstract questions on paper say very little about how someone reasons when time is short and the stakes are real.

**3. Good candidates quit halfway**
Long, repetitive assessments lose people before they finish.

**4. Manual scoring slows down hiring**
Your HR team spends days scoring, compiling and formatting results by hand, while candidates wait.

### 4a. Personality · DEUS Discover (`#personality`)

Eyebrow: Module 1 of 4

## DEUS Discover: Personality Test

Sub-line: Know your candidates before the interview.

> Note: F64 (R15a). The sub-line is F23's line (approved 2026-10-16), formerly Discover's closer.

Discover puts candidates inside an RPG-style story and asks them to make decisions as it unfolds. The way they handle each situation builds a detailed picture of their personality and competencies, measured against the framework your organization already uses.

**A questionnaire asks people who they are. A story shows you.**

Most personality tests ask candidates to rate themselves: "I enjoy meeting new people", agree or disagree. It's easy to work out what the employer wants to hear.

Discover takes a different route. It's a digital situational judgment test, delivered as a narrative game. Candidates face situations and dilemmas as the story moves on, and choose what to do next. There's no obvious right answer to aim for, so what you see is closer to how they really behave at work.

**What it measures**

**32** personality subtraits
**24** competency traits
**Custom** competencies from your own framework

Built on the **Big Five (OCEAN)** model of personality and a **Universal Competency Framework**, and written up as an in-depth qualitative analysis, not just a row of scores.

**Mapped to each role**

**Scenarios from your world**

## Cognitive Assessment

Short, focused games that measure how fast and how accurately someone processes information. Because they're timed and interactive, they capture speed and consistency as well as correct answers.

| Odd One Out | Pattern recognition |
| Mallet Math | Arithmetic and problem solving |
| Object Turn | Spatial reasoning |
| Match Blast | Logical reasoning |
| Speed Sorting | Cognitive agility |

- Answers can be coached or memorized.

- A benchmark for learning agility.

### Games, built like serious psychometric assessments.

> Note: F69 (R15a): "psychometric" added for search; for the owner to approve with D9.

We didn't invent new theories. We built on proven ones.

**Personality: the Big Five** · The Big Five (OCEAN) model and a Universal Competency Framework. *(→ #personality)*
**Thinking: cognitive assessment** · Classical frameworks for processing speed, memory, reasoning and attention. *(→ #cognitive)*
**Behavior: neuroscience paradigms** · Behavioral tasks adapted from neuroscience research, revealing risk-taking, motivation, effort and emotion recognition through what people do rather than what they say. *(→ #neuro)*

> Note: D8 (R15a): the neuroscience card took over the line removed from the Neuroscience module.

**Four phases, before a single candidate plays.**

Every assessment goes through the same development process, guided by psychometricians and industrial and organizational psychology researchers.

**1. Theory**
We start from what the research says the trait or ability is, and how it shows up in behavior.

**2. Item development**
We design the game mechanics and scenarios that will draw out that behavior.

**3. Validation**
We test whether the game measures what it's meant to, including against traditional tests.

**4. Norming**
We benchmark results against a reference population, so every score means something.

**Validated**
Our cognitive games have been validated against traditional cognitive tests, showing convergent validity: people who score high on one tend to score high on the other. The difference is that ours are faster, more engaging, and much harder to coach for.

> **[VERIFY]** Any validation figures the team can publish (sample sizes, correlations, reliability). Don't add numbers until they're confirmed.

**Normed for Indonesia**
Many assessments used in Indonesia are benchmarked against Western populations. That means a candidate's score is measured against people with different schooling, different language and different cultural reference points. Our assessments are normed on the Indonesian population. When a report says a candidate is in the 80th percentile, it means compared with Indonesians, which is the comparison that matters for your hiring.

## Questions HR teams ask about game-based assessment.

**Do candidates need to install anything?**
No. The assessment runs in a mobile or desktop browser. Candidates just need a stable internet connection.

Scores and reports are generated as soon as each candidate finishes. Personality profiles are ready the same day or the next.

Every session starts with device checks and anti-cheat logic. And because the games measure behavior, not answers, there's very little to look up or memorize.
