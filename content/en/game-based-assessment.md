## A smarter assessment for better hiring results.

Game-based assessment measures how candidates think and behave through short games instead of a questionnaire. The DEUS suite has four modules, Personality, Cognitive, Neuroscience and Simulation, normed on the Indonesian population. It runs in any phone or laptop browser with no app, and results are ready the same day or the next. Companies in Indonesia use it for high-volume hiring and talent decisions.

## All your talent assessments on one platform.

Use any module on its own or combine them. All four run on the same platform, and their reports are produced automatically.

### 3a. Personality (`#personality`)

### DEUS Discover: Personality Test

Candidates play through an RPG-style story and decide what to do at each turn. Their choices show how they tend to behave at work.

**32** personality subtraits and **24** competency traits, based on the Big Five (OCEAN) model. You can add competencies from your own framework.

Screen: the Discover story scene (`images.discoverFestival`, current alt text).

### 3b. Cognitive (`#cognitive`)

### Cognitive Assessment

Seven short, timed games that measure how quickly and accurately someone processes information.

**7 games:** processing speed, working memory, pattern recognition, arithmetic, spatial reasoning, logical reasoning and cognitive agility.

Screen: Objective Shooter (`images.gameObjectiveShooter`, current alt text; the hero already shows Mallet Math).

### 3c. Neuroscience (`#neuro`)

### Neuroscience Assessment

Six games adapted from tasks used in neuroscience research. They pick up behavior people don't consciously control.

**6 games:** risk-taking, planning, motivation, effort allocation, attention to detail and emotion recognition.

Screen: the Balloon Game (`images.gameBalloon`, current alt text).

### 3d. Simulation (`#simulation`)

### Simulation Assessment

Two timed business cases. In one, candidates review information and write a business report; in the other, they split a budget while a timer runs. Both can be set in your industry.

**2 scenarios:** analytical thinking, financial and business acumen, diagnostic information gathering and written synthesis.

Screen: Business Operations (`images.gbaSimulation`, alt "Business Operations: a mini case analysis with a calculator.").

## Questions HR teams ask about game-based assessment.

**How is it different from a traditional psychometric test (psikotes)?**
It measures what candidates do, not what they say about themselves. Answers to a traditional psikotes can be looked up and rehearsed. In our games, every choice, every hesitation and every reaction under time pressure becomes data, so candidates can't fake how they play. The games are faster and more engaging than a long questionnaire, and scoring is automatic instead of by hand.

**Is game-based assessment scientifically valid?**
Yes. Our games are built like serious psychometric assessments, on the Big Five, classical cognitive frameworks and neuroscience research. Each one goes through theory, item development, validation and norming, guided by psychometricians and industrial and organizational psychology researchers. Our cognitive games have been validated against traditional cognitive tests, and every assessment is normed on the Indonesian population.

**What can we use it for?**
Hiring and talent decisions, from your MT intake to your next generation of leaders. Use it for recruitment, Management Trainee intakes, competency mapping and leadership identification. It also checks fit before a promotion, rotation or transfer (mutasi), and shows where training budget should go for high-potentials and succession candidates.

**Which modules should we choose?**
Choose the modules that match the decision. For recruitment, Personality and Cognitive give you a clear, defensible ranking. For a Management Trainee intake, use Cognitive and Neuroscience to shortlist, then Simulation for the final round. To find future leaders, the Neuroscience module's data on risk, motivation and EQ is especially useful. You can start with one module and add others later.

**Do candidates need to install anything?**
No. The assessment runs in a mobile or desktop browser. Candidates just need a stable internet connection.

**How quickly do we get results?**
Scores and reports are generated as soon as each candidate finishes. Personality profiles are ready the same day or the next.

**What reports do we get?**
You get an individual report for each candidate, a batch report for the whole intake, and an interactive dashboard your hiring committee can filter and sort. They're produced automatically when each candidate finishes. The individual report shows an overall score and percentile, each competency, strengths, development areas and a hiring recommendation, as a PDF or a live link. Personality also comes with a written profile and how closely the candidate matches the role.

**How many candidates can it handle at once?**
As many as you need, at the same time. A national telecom group used DEUS to assess more than 1,000 Management Trainee candidates across 30+ provinces at once, with results within a day, two years in a row. The platform sends the invitations and reminders, tracks progress and generates the reports, so a nationwide intake doesn't mean adding people to your HR team.

**Can candidates cheat?**
Every session starts with device checks and anti-cheat logic. And because the games measure behavior, not answers, there's very little to look up or memorize.

**Can we assess against our own competency framework?**
Yes. Results can be expressed in your organization's language, mapped to the competencies each role needs, and set in scenarios from your industry.

**Is it designed for Indonesian candidates?**
Yes. Our assessments are normed on the Indonesian population, so scores are compared against relevant local benchmarks.

**Can we try it first?**
Yes. Ask for a free trial, or start with a pilot in one division.
