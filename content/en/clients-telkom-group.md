## 3. The challenge

Eyebrow: The challenge

## A national intake, on a recruitment timeline.

Telkom Group's Management Trainee program recruits for Telkom Indonesia and six of its subsidiaries, with candidates spread across the country. The selection team needed to assess more than a thousand people in over 30 provinces at once, fairly and consistently, and have results ready quickly enough to keep the process moving.

---

## 4. What DEUS did

Eyebrow: What we did

## One assessment, run everywhere at once.

DEUS ran its game-based assessment for every candidate simultaneously, in more than 30 provinces. Candidates took it in their browser, without traveling to a test center, and results were delivered within one day.

The following year, Telkom Group ran its Management Trainee selection with DEUS again.
