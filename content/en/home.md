Eyebrow: Why DEUS exists

## Most people decisions still run on thin, scattered evidence.

> Note: heading from D6 (F45, 2026-10-22); the paragraphs show both, thin (a CV, a practiced psychotest) and scattered (five spreadsheets).

A CV, an hour-long interview, and a psychotest the candidate practiced online the night before. A year later, an appraisal pieced together from five spreadsheets, and a bonus nobody can quite explain.

Companies say people are their most important asset. Then they make decisions about them with the weakest data they have.

**We started DEUS in 2022 to give those decisions better evidence.**

DEUS Human Capital Services is an HR technology company in Indonesia, founded in 2022. More than 40 brands use DEUS, across retail, telecommunications, education, FMCG, F&B, electronics, fashion, healthcare, manufacturing and finance.

## 9. Why DEUS

Eyebrow: Why DEUS

## What you can expect from us.

**1. Evidence over gut feel**
A candidate's games, an employee's KPIs and a child's minigames are all scored in one structured way, the same for everyone, so you can put people side by side and decide on results instead of memory or a first impression.

**2. Fits what you already use**
Candidates and children play in a browser, on a phone or a laptop, with no app to download. Enhance can work alongside the HRIS, ERP and payroll systems you already have.

**3. Ready for volume**
Invite a whole intake from one spreadsheet, follow every plant and branch in one place, or see a whole class on one dashboard. Results are calculated by the system, so a bigger group doesn't add manual work for your team.

**4. We stay involved**
Start small, with a free trial, a pilot or a short review of how you work today. After that, we help design your KPIs, train your team, review each cycle with you, and talk parents through their child's results one on one.
