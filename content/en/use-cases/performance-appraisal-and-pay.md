## 2. The problem

Eyebrow: The problem

## Ratings in one spreadsheet, bonuses in another.

When appraisal results and rewards are worked out in different places, nobody can show how one led to the other.

**1. Scattered data**
KPIs and appraisal results sit across Excel and disconnected systems. It's the week before calibration, and HR is still pulling scores together.

**2. Inconsistent standards**
Two managers rate the same performance differently. Nobody notices until calibration, if at all.

**3. Disconnected rewards**
The bonus gets finalized from a spreadsheet that was never checked against the actual performance ratings.

**4. No answer to "why?"**
An employee asks why a colleague got a bigger bonus. No one can explain it with objective evidence.

## 3. How DEUS does it

Eyebrow: How DEUS does it

## How Enhance connects the rating to the reward.

**1. Set the framework**
Customize KPIs, competencies, weights, formulas, periods and measures by role and level.

**2. Review performance**
One structured self-review and appraisal process for everyone, tracked from company level down to teams and individuals.

**3. Calibrate results**
Review evaluations across managers, departments or plants for consistency. The calibration matrix shows everyone's evaluations together, so ratings that don't follow the same standard stand out.

**4. Map talent**
A nine-box grid plots KPI results against competency results, so you can spot high performers, performance gaps and development potential.

**5. Turn results into pay decisions**
Connect the calibrated results to incentives, bonuses, salary increases and development. Set up each bonus cycle and who takes part, group similar performance into similar rewards, and plan raises beside the performance behind them.

Enhance can complement your existing HRIS, ERP, payroll and operational systems, without necessarily replacing them. How data flows between them is confirmed during scoping.

## 6. FAQ

Eyebrow: FAQ

## Questions about linking appraisal to pay.

**How does Enhance link performance appraisal to pay?**
It feeds calibrated appraisal results straight into bonus, incentive and salary increase decisions. KPIs, appraisals, calibration and talent mapping sit in one platform, and the results flow into bonus cycles, reward grouping and raise planning. So when an employee asks why a colleague got a bigger bonus, there's measured performance behind the answer.

**Why calibrate before deciding bonuses?**
Because managers, departments or plants may rate the same performance differently. Calibration reviews evaluations across them for consistency before the results reach rewards. In Enhance, the calibration matrix shows everyone's evaluations together, so ratings that don't follow the same standard stand out. Only then do the results feed bonus, incentive and salary decisions.

**Do we have to replace our HRIS or payroll system?**
No. Enhance works alongside your existing HRIS, ERP, payroll and operational systems, and doesn't necessarily require replacing any of them. Integration, data flow and configuration are adapted to your organization's processes, and we agree how data moves between the systems during scoping.

**Can we use our own KPIs and competencies?**
Yes. You set the KPIs, competencies, weights, formulas, periods and measures, and they can differ by role and level. Performance is then tracked from company level down to each team and individual, and every employee goes through the same structured self-review and appraisal process.

**We don't have a KPI framework yet. Can we still start?**
Yes. Plenty of companies start there. We can help you design KPIs and competencies that fit each role and level, often beginning with a KPI-mapping workshop. The first step is a short review of how your appraisals work today, and from there we recommend a workshop, a pilot or a tailored trial.
