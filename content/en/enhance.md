## 5. From rating to reward *(new)*

Eyebrow: From rating to reward

## A rating only matters if it changes what happens next.

In many companies, the appraisal ends with a score and the bonus is worked out somewhere else, in a different spreadsheet, by different people. Enhance keeps it in one line: from the rating, through calibration, to the reward. So every bonus and every raise can be traced back to the performance behind it.

**1. Set up the bonus cycle**
Define each bonus cycle and the rules that link it to performance results.

**2. Choose who takes part**
Decide which employees are included in each cycle.

**3. Group rewards fairly**
Group employees so that similar performance leads to similar rewards.

**4. Plan salary increases**
Review salary increases side by side with the performance behind them.

**So when an employee asks "why?", there's an answer.**

## 12. FAQ *(new)*

Eyebrow: FAQ

## Questions we hear before every first call.

**Do we have to replace our HRIS or payroll system?**
No. Enhance works alongside your existing HRIS, ERP, payroll and operational systems. We agree how data flows between them during scoping.

**Can we use our own KPIs and competencies?**
Yes. You set the KPIs, competencies, weights, formulas, periods and measures, and they can differ by role and level.

**We don't have a proper KPI framework yet. Is that a problem?**
Not at all. Plenty of companies start there. We can help you design it, often beginning with a KPI-mapping workshop.

**Does it work if we have several plants or branches?**
Yes. Performance is tracked from company level down to each team and individual, so plant and operations leaders can see how every site is doing.

**How does Enhance connect performance to pay?**
Appraisal and calibration results feed straight into bonus cycles, reward grouping and salary increase decisions, so rewards rest on measured performance.

**How do we get started?**
With a short review of how your appraisals work today. From there we recommend the right next step: a KPI-mapping workshop, a pilot or a tailored trial.

**What support do we get after go-live?**
User training, a review after each cycle, and hands-on help whenever you need it.
