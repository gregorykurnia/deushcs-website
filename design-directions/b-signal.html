Principles

What makes Signal different from the current guide.

  • Show the measurement. Every product section carries a real readout, chart or log, never a stock illustration.
  • Grids you can see. Content sits in cells that share 1px borders, like a spec sheet.
  • Cobalt replaces orange as the brand action. Orange becomes the data highlight, so it always means "look here".
  • Semibold, narrow headlines. Confident without shouting.
  • No decoration layer. No glows, no dot grids. The one texture is the faint grid on the closing band.
  • Tabular numbers everywhere. Scores, times and percentages line up.
