Daylight

Principles

What makes Daylight different from the current guide.

Space is the luxury

144px between sections, centered headers, never more than one idea per screen.

Product in the light

Real screens float over the sky gradient with soft, deep shadows. No laptop frames.

Quiet type, big size

SemiBold instead of ExtraBold. A grey second line instead of an orange word.

Pills and soft corners

Buttons, nav and chips are pills; panels step from 16 to 36px radius.

One warm note

Ember appears once per view, as a dot or marker, so the blue never feels cold.

Interaction shows the story

Hire, develop, reward becomes tabs that swap the product screen, not three static columns.
