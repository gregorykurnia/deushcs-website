# SEO trends: where things stand (October 2026)

Research notes, written 2026-10-23. Is SEO still worth it for a B2B company site like deushcs.com, and what has changed?

**Short answer:** yes, SEO still matters, but what it means has changed a lot. People now get answers from AI (Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity) without clicking through. The goal is no longer only to rank: it's to be the source those AI answers quote and link to.

> Most figures below come from SEO vendors and their blogs, and they don't agree with each other. The direction is consistent, but don't plan around any single number.

## What's changed

1. **People get answers without clicking.** Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode answer many questions on the results page. When an AI Overview appears, the top organic result gets roughly a third fewer clicks (Ahrefs: 34.5% lower). Studies range from 34% to 61% fewer clicks depending on position. The often-quoted "93% of AI Mode searches get no click" hasn't been traced to its original source, so treat it with caution.
2. **B2B buyers start in AI tools.** HubSpot reports that almost two-thirds of buyers now begin research in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and similar tools instead of Google.
3. **The new name is GEO (generative engine optimization).** It means being the source an AI answer quotes or links to. Google's own 2026 guidance says this is still SEO: the basics haven't changed, only where you show up. Some call the wider idea "Search Everywhere Optimization": being findable wherever buyers look.
4. **Fewer visitors, but better ones.** Several sources report that visitors arriving from AI answers convert better, and that a site linked inside an AI Overview can get more clicks (one source: 22% more) than a plain #1 ranking.
5. **Low-value AI content is being punished.** Google hasn't run a broad core update since May. Another update is widely expected between late October and mid-November 2026, aimed at sites that publish lots of thin AI-written pages.
6. **Google is sending some traffic back out.** It's adding more links inside AI Mode and AI Overviews and rolling out "Preferred Sources", where users choose which sites they want to see more of (usage up more than 7x since the start of 2026).
7. **Rank tracking is less reliable.** Nozzle reports that since 20 September some Google results served to automated requests have been wrong (5–11% a day in October), so rank-tracker numbers can mislead.

### Numbers that disagree

- **How often AI Overviews appear:** one source says 4.5–12.5% of all searches; another says up to 80–88% of informational searches in some industries.
- **Click recovery:** a Seer Interactive study (2.43 billion impressions, 53 brands) found click rates on AI Overview queries fell from 1.76% to 0.61% by September 2025, then recovered to 2.4% by February 2026.
- **Commercial searches still click:** for "buy/hire" type searches, the #1 organic result still drives a lot of clicks.

## What this means for deushcs.com

### Still needed (the basics)

- Fast pages, clean structure, one clear page per service.
- Structured data (Organization, Service, FAQ).
- Redirects from old URLs. The Task 7.3b redirects from the WordPress site already protect the rankings it built up.

### Newer, and worth doing

- **Content worth quoting.** AI tools cite specific facts, named experts, real case results and original data. Pages that say what everyone else says don't get cited. The assessment work could produce findings nobody else has.
- **Clear question-and-answer sections,** such as "What is a game-based assessment?", written so an AI can lift the answer whole.
- **Being visible beyond the site.** AI answers lean heavily on LinkedIn, industry directories, press mentions and reviews. A strong LinkedIn company page and mentions in Indonesian HR media help as much as anything on the site itself.
- **Bing Webmaster Tools.** ChatGPT's search has been reported to rely on Bing's index, so register the site there as well as in Google Search Console once it goes live and indexing is switched on (Task 7.5).
- **Content in Indonesian.** There's less competition for Indonesian-language HR topics, so it's easier to become the cited source.

### New things to measure

- Traffic from AI tools (chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com) in analytics.
- Growth in people searching for "DEUS" by name.
- Spot checks: ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity questions like "best assessment provider in Indonesia" and note whether DEUS comes up.

### What to skip

- Publishing lots of AI-written blog posts to rank for many keywords. That's the content the expected update is aimed at.
